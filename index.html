The Career Maze

Your associate degree is a journey full of decisions. Pick a door, then at every fork choose what you do next, one choice per semester. After four semesters, step through a graduation arch to see where your choices led.

ComputerWASD or arrow keys to walk. Mouse to look. Click to interact.
Phone or tabletJoystick to walk. Drag to look. Tap signs and doors.
VR headsetTap Enter VR. Push the right stick forward, or hold the trigger, to teleport.

How to play

GoalWalk through any door. At each fork, a sign shows two choices for that semester: walk into the hall you choose. After four semesters, step through a graduation arch to see where your choices led.
ComputerWASD or arrows to walk, Shift to jog, mouse to look, click signs and doors. Esc frees the mouse.
Phone or tabletJoystick (bottom left) to walk, drag anywhere else to look, tap signs and doors.
VR headsetPush the right stick forward or hold a trigger to aim a teleport, then release. Right stick left or right turns. Press A or X for comfort settings.
ShortcutsM sound on or off, L back to the lobby, H this help.
